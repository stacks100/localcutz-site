Questions

Good to Know

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are always welcome. Calling ahead saves you the wait, especially on Saturdays and after school.

How long is the wait for a walk-in?

Usually under 20 minutes on weekdays. Saturdays get busy, so call first and we'll tell you the wait.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, any age. Jay is great with first haircuts and kids who don't love sitting still.

What if I'm running late?

Call us. We hold your spot for 10 minutes, then take the next walk-in and fit you in after.

Do you do designs?

Yes. Hard parts and freestyle designs can be added to any cut for $5. Bring a photo if you have one.
